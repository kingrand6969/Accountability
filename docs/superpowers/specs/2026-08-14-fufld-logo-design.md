# FUFLD Logo Design

## Objective

Replace the dated metallic FUFLD logo with a clean, contemporary identity for an employer-funded employee-support platform. The identity should feel human, credible, and technologically capable while remaining recognizable to existing audiences.

## Approved Direction: Fulfilment Loop

Create a compact circular emblem made from four flowing, interdependent segments. The four segments represent FUFLD's support categories: enjoyment, wellness, convenience, and growth. Their shared centre represents the employee and expresses the care-to-fulfilment-to-contribution model.

Pair the emblem with a custom uppercase `FUFLD` wordmark. The lettering should be geometric and confident, with subtly softened corners so it does not feel cold or institutional. The emblem and wordmark must work both together and independently.

## Visual Language

- Primary colour: deep navy, approximately `#071B3D`.
- Accent colour: vivid blue, approximately `#1677FF`.
- Optional highlight: clear sky blue, approximately `#55B8FF`.
- Flat colour only; no chrome, bevels, faux 3D, or decorative shadows.
- Strong negative space and simple geometry suitable for small digital sizes.
- Overall character: modern HR-tech with a warm, care-first undertone.

## Deliverables

- Primary horizontal logo on a light background.
- Reversed logo on a navy background.
- Standalone emblem suitable for an app icon or favicon.
- A visual concept sheet showing the system and colour palette.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The wordmark reads exactly `FUFLD`.
- The mark remains legible at small sizes.
- The four-part idea is perceptible without becoming literal or busy.
- The result is distinct from the existing metallic monogram.
- The identity works in one colour as well as the blue palette.
- No tagline, gradients, mockup scenery, or unrelated symbols appear in the core logo.
