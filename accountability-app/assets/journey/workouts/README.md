# Journey workout photography

These 64 local WebP assets were generated specifically for AccountAbility with OpenAI image generation, then cropped from four exact 4×4 equal-cell contact sheets. Each crop removes the straight white gutters and is optimized at 368×368 for the mobile hero.

Attribution for every entry: `OpenAI generated for AccountAbility` / `OpenAI`.

Generation mode for all four sheets: `Use case: photorealistic-natural`. The final prompts specified premium photorealistic editorial fitness photography, diverse adults, varied gym/home/outdoor settings, natural anatomy, safe form, inclusive representation, straight gutters with no cross-cell content, and no text, logos, watermarks, or duplicate scenes.

- `push/`: exact 4×4 equal-cell PUSH sheet covering bench press, incline dumbbell press, chest press, overhead press, push-ups, dips, triceps work, landmine press, and related pushing movements.
- `pull/`: exact 4×4 equal-cell PULL sheet covering pull-ups, pulldowns, cable and dumbbell rows, barbell rows, deadlifts, face pulls, reverse flyes, and curls.
- `legs/`: exact 4×4 equal-cell LEGS sheet covering squat variations, lunges, leg press, hack squat, Romanian deadlift, hip thrust, hamstring curls, leg extensions, calf work, step-ups, and kettlebell swings.
- `general/`: exact 4×4 equal-cell GENERAL sheet covering cardio, conditioning, training atmosphere, mobility, recovery, consistency, and group workout imagery.

The source contact sheets are retained in the local Codex image-generation output for provenance and are not bundled into the app.
